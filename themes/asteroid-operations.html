HARROW / DATA

A first-person helmet HUD on the dorsal hull of the freighter HARROW, station-keeping at asteroid Bennu. The hull, plate wear, spiral fit and suit telemetry are a fictional reconstruction. Real open data is layered alongside.

SCENE

  • Bennu shape: NASA Science / PDS public-domain shape model (decimated).
  • Bennu orbit/range/velocity: JPL SBDB + Horizons (baked).
  • Star field: HYG Database v4.4, CC BY-SA 4.0, Astronexus.
  • Freighter and hull: clean-room procedural reconstruction; position is simulated.

LIVE DATA

  • NASA DSN Now — real deep-space link contacts.
  • NOAA SWPC — solar wind and Kp.
  • JPL CAD/SBDB — next NEO close approaches (baked).
