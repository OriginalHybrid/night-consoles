GYRE WATCH / DATA

A storm-forecasting aerostat display. The hero is a real satellite/radar view; the storm track and forecast come from live NHC data via MaverickWX.

LIVE

  • MaverickWX active storms and manifests, sourced from NOAA/NHC and JTWC
  • NASA GIBS GOES-East, GOES-West and Himawari infrared imagery
  • Open-Meteo upper-air and surface weather at the aerostat

ARCHIVE

If no active storm or live imagery is available, Hurricane Ian 2022 is shown from a NOAA/NASA GIBS VIIRS brightness-temperature reconstruction. It is labelled ARCHIVE.

The hero opens on the archive reconstruction. Use the LIVE button in the header to load the current storm, and ARCHIVE to return.

STYLE

Infrared cloud-top brightness is mapped to a radar-like dBZ palette for the GYRE WATCH plan view. It is a stylised satellite view, not measured radar reflectivity.

MODEL PANELS

RHI, ensemble, lightning, barographs, hodograph and wind-profile panels are reconstructions derived from the storm intensity, wind radii, official forecast, and aerostat observations. They are labelled MODEL or EST; they are not measured radar or in-situ data.
