DECLINATION / DATA

A clean-room geomagnetic observatory scene. The field geometry is IGRF-14; the storm animation is driven by live NOAA SWPC data.

LIVE / SNAPSHOT

  • NOAA SWPC solar-wind magnetic field and speed summaries
  • NOAA SWPC Kp, Dst, R/S/G scales and alerts
  • NOAA SWPC OVATION aurora probability
  • USGS Geomagnetism observatory BOU: H, D, Z, F

MODEL

  • IGRF-14 coefficients, epoch 2025.0 (IAGA / NOAA NCEI)
  • Dip poles and port declinations computed from IGRF-14
  • 260 field lines traced with RK4 and baked into the scene

ASSETS

Earth texture: NASA Blue Marble / Visible Earth (public domain). Field-line cage is a visual magnetosphere, not a full MHD simulation.

Flags: ?sim=1 uses the baked snapshot; ?motion=off freezes the animated hero and panels.
